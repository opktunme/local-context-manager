# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language, harassment, doxxing, and publishing another person's private data are not.

Examples of positive participation include being specific and kind in reviews, accepting correction, respecting different levels of experience, and keeping discussion focused on the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security-advisory reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
